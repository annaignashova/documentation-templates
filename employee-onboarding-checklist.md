# Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Before the First Day

- Prepare workstation and required accounts
- Confirm schedule and assigned supervisor
- Prepare onboarding materials
- Verify access to internal documentation

## First Day

- Welcome and introduction
- Team introduction
- Workplace tour
- Review basic policies
- Explain communication channels
- Introduce daily responsibilities

## Training

- Review work instructions
- Demonstrate standard procedures
- Explain safety requirements
- Observe practical tasks
- Answer questions

## Knowledge Check

- Confirm understanding of key procedures
- Review common scenarios
- Identify areas requiring additional support

## Follow-up

- Collect feedback
- Schedule additional training if needed
- Review progress after the first week
